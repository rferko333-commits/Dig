Dig prototype

Visibility is limited to open ground and adjacent walls (dug areas remain visible). Tap units to select them (or tap a unit icon on the right bar to select all of that type). Miners prioritize enemy miners over mole people; tap an enemy while units are selected to attack it, or tap ground drops to collect them. Tap a placed building in-game to show its ▲ upgrade arrow (3 upgrades each, 2× cost per tier). Diggers drill 2 tiles in front of them, break gray hard rock, and gather Rock. Dark Bedrock can only be cleared slowly by a crewed Laser. Exposed lava slowly erodes floor—use Dump Trucks to build rock walls, fill water into floor, or pour Water from a Water Plant to dig moats around your crystal! Open Editor to create, save, and play custom levels.
